TapCash Base Sepolia

Tap your Ledger. Pay in USDC.

Hold a Ledger Flex to an iPhone, approve on the Flex, and USDC moves on Base. The receiver needs only Safari, the payer needs no ETH, and the key never leaves the secure element.

Open the app View on GitHub

Works on a real Ledger Flex today, on the Base Sepolia testnet.

How it works

Four steps, two taps. No app to install on either side.

  1. 1Enter the amount

    The receiver types the amount on the iPhone page in Safari.

  2. 2Tap the Flex

    Hold the iPhone near the Ledger Flex. The request travels over NFC.

  3. 3Check and approve

    The Flex shows the amount and recipient. Approve on the Flex, then tap again.

  4. 4USDC arrives

    The iPhone checks the signature, pays the gas, and the USDC lands.

Three ways to pay

You choose how much the Flex does on its own. Every mode is set on the Flex itself.

Default

Manual

Two taps. Every payment is reviewed and approved on the Flex.

Spending limits

Budget

Set limits on the Flex: per payment, in total, and number of payments. Payments within them go on one tap. Anything over falls back to manual.

One uncapped transaction

Card

Turn it on right before you pay. The next tap pays any amount, then it turns off after that payment or after one minute.

Security

The Flex holds the key and decides what gets signed. The iPhone only asks.

Keys stay on the Flex

The signing key lives in the Flex's secure element and never leaves it.

You see what you sign

The Flex shows the amount and recipient, and builds the message it signs itself. It never signs a hash from the phone.

Pinned to one token

Each signature is an EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization for USDC on Base Sepolia, so it can't be replayed on another chain.

One approval, one payment

Each approval yields exactly one signature. The payer pays no gas.

Where the human decides

Manual
You approve every payment on the Flex.
Budget
You approve the limits once. At most what's left of the budget can go without review.
Card
You approve one payment of any amount, for up to one minute. Turn it on only at the moment you pay.
Sandbox: test money

Cash out to a card

Received USDC can become a virtual Visa card. TapCash sends the USDC to a cash-out address, reads the amount from the on-chain receipt, and issues a card with that spend limit.

Today this runs in the Increase sandbox, with a test card and test money. In production the same step would go through a card issuer.
